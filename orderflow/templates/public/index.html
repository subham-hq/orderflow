How it works

Up and running in three steps

  1. 01

    Add your catalog

    Add products one at a time, or import a CSV file with each product's SKU, name, unit, price and GST rate.

  2. 02

    Invite your clients

    Each buyer gets an email invitation, chooses a password and sees only your catalog and their own orders.

  3. 03

    Review and fulfil

    New orders arrive in your workspace and your inbox. Approve, reject with a reason or mark fulfilled; the client is emailed each time.
